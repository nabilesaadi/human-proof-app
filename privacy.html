Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 04, 2026

1. Data Sovereignty & Protection

At HumanProof, we operate under a "Privacy by Design" framework. Our infrastructure is built to ensure that your intellectual property remains yours. Unlike traditional databases, we utilize cryptographic hashing, which means we do not need to store, read, or analyze your full text to certify it.

2. Information We Collect

When you use our certification engine, we collect minimal technical data necessary for the verification process:

  • Cryptographic Hashes: A SHA-256 digital fingerprint of your content.
  • Metadata: Timestamps of the certification and a 50-character preview for your own identification.
  • Usage Data: IP addresses and browser types for security monitoring and fraud prevention.

3. Cookies & Advertising (AdSense Compliance)

HumanProof uses cookies to enhance user experience. We also participate in the Google AdSense program. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site. Google's use of the DART cookie enables it to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google ad and content network privacy policy.

4. Third-Party Services (Lemon Squeezy)

Payment processing is handled by our partner, Lemon Squeezy. They operate as a Merchant of Record and have their own privacy protocols regarding your credit card information and billing address. We do not store your financial data on our servers.

5. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Users in the European Union and California have specific rights regarding their data, including the right to access, delete, or port their certification records. To exercise these rights, please contact our data protection officer.
