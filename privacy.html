1. Our Commitment to Privacy

At HumanProof, privacy is not an afterthought; it is our primary technology. We understand the sensitivity of your creative and professional content. Our system is engineered to provide absolute certainty of authorship while maintaining total confidentiality.

The Zero-Knowledge Pledge:

We do not store, read, or process your full text on our servers. All sensitive data remains within your local environment until the cryptographic hash is generated.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect the minimum amount of data required to provide a verifiable timestamping service:

Cryptographic Fingerprints

We store a SHA-256 hash, a non-reversible mathematical string that represents your text without containing any of the original words.

Verification Metadata

This includes the exact timestamp of certification and a short 50-character preview for your record-keeping.

4. Third-Party Partners

To maintain a professional service, we partner with industry leaders in advertising and payments:

  • Google AdSense: Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your visit. The DART cookie allows Google and its partners to serve ads based on your internet usage. You can manage these settings in your Google Account.
  • Lemon Squeezy: As our Merchant of Record, Lemon Squeezy processes all financial transactions. They collect billing information necessary for tax compliance and payment processing.

5. Your Global Data Rights

Depending on your location (GDPR in Europe, CCPA in California), you have specific rights over your data:

R

Right to Access: You can request a copy of all certification hashes linked to your identity.

E

Right to Erasure: You may request the permanent removal of your certification records from our database.
