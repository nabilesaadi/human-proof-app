Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 10, 2026

1. Our Core Privacy Principle: Zero-Knowledge

At Human-Integrity, privacy is not a setting; it is our fundamental technical architecture. We operate on a Zero-Knowledge Principle[cite: 140, 148]. This means our infrastructure is designed so that we never have access to, nor the ability to view, your original intellectual property[cite: 141].

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

Unlike traditional cloud services, HumanProof does not require you to upload your documents to our servers[cite: 146].

  • No Content Storage: We never see or store your manuscripts, research papers, or raw text[cite: 146, 151].
  • No Files: No files are transmitted to our infrastructure during the certification process[cite: 146].

3. Data We Process: Local Hashing

When you use the HumanProof console, the cryptographic transformation occurs entirely within your own web browser (Client-Side Execution)[cite: 143, 144].

  • SHA-256 Fingerprint: The only data transmitted to our servers is the 64-character alphanumeric hash (the digital fingerprint)[cite: 149, 150].
  • Non-Reversibility: This hash is a one-way mathematical function. It is impossible for Human-Integrity or any third party to reverse-engineer the hash to reveal your original content[cite: 133, 150].

4. Information We Store

To provide a verifiable proof of existence, we record the following metadata in our permanent registry:

  • The SHA-256 digital fingerprint[cite: 163].
  • A synchronized universal timestamp[cite: 164].
  • Information related to your Professional Seal (if purchased) for certificate generation.

5. Security of Intellectual Property

By decentralizing the hashing process to your local device, we eliminate the risk of a "single point of failure"[cite: 152, 153]. Even in the event of a server breach, your intellectual property remains secure because it was never in our possession[cite: 154].
