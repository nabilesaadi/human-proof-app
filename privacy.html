Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Commitment to Privacy

At HumanProof, your privacy is our core foundation. We use cryptographic principles to verify content without ever needing to see or store your full intellectual property.

2. Zero-Knowledge Hashing

When you use our tool, your text is processed locally in your browser. A SHA-256 hash (digital fingerprint) is created. Only this hash and a minimal preview are stored in our secure database. This ensures your full work remains entirely private.

3. Data Protection Rights

Under global data protection standards (GDPR/CCPA), you maintain the right to access, correct, or request the erasure of any certification records linked to your identifiers.
