01. Privacy as Infrastructure

At **Human-Integrity**, we believe privacy is a fundamental human right. Our flagship product, **HumanProof**, is engineered with a "Privacy-by-Design" architecture. We do not just protect your data; we ensure we never have access to it in the first place.

The Zero-Knowledge Protocol

Our technology utilizes local-side SHA-256 cryptographic hashing. This means the mathematical fingerprint of your content is generated within your browser environment. Your original text is never transmitted to our servers.

02. Data Acquisition & Processing

We collect only the essential metadata required to maintain an immutable public registry:

  • Cryptographic Hashes

    The 64-character SHA-256 string representing your content. This is a non-reversible digital anchor.

  • Temporal Metadata

    Precise atomic timestamps of certification and short 50-character previews for administrative indexing.

03. Ecosystem Partnerships

To provide a global-scale service, we collaborate with industry-leading infrastructure partners:

04. Global Regulatory Compliance

We adhere to international data protection standards, including GDPR (Europe) and CCPA (California). Your rights include:

A
Right to Access & Portability

Request a structured report of all cryptographic certifications linked to your identity.

E
Right to Erasure

Request the permanent removal of your certification metadata from our registry.
