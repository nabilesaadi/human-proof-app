1. Understanding Cookies

To ensure our cryptographic certification engine works seamlessly across all browsers, HumanProof uses "Cookies". These are small data files stored on your device that help us recognize your session and maintain secure infrastructure standards.

2. How We Categorize Cookies

We use three primary types of cookies to operate our global platform:

Category Purpose Duration
Essential Security monitoring and session persistence for certification hashes. Session
Analytics Understanding traffic patterns to optimize global server load. 1 Year
Advertising Personalizing your experience through the Google AdSense network. 90 Days

3. Google AdSense & DART Cookies

As a professional publisher, HumanProof integrates with the Google AdSense network. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to our website or other websites.

"Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to your users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet."

Action Required:

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.

4. Payment Processing Cookies

When you purchase a Professional Seal, our partner **Lemon Squeezy** (Merchant of Record) sets specific cookies to handle the checkout process and fraud detection. These cookies do not store your full text content and are strictly for financial transaction security.

5. Controlling Your Data

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings preferences. However, if you limit the ability of websites to set cookies, you may worsen your overall user experience, as it will no longer be personalized to you. It may also stop you from saving customized settings like certification session data.
