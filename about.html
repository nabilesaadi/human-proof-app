The Integrity Mandate

Founded in 2026, Human-Integrity emerged as a response to the rapid expansion of generative artificial intelligence. While AI offers immense potential, it has also introduced a fundamental crisis of authenticity.

At the core of our philosophy is a simple belief: human original thought is a unique asset that deserves immutable protection. HumanProof was developed as the technical interface to provide this protection through advanced cryptographic timestamping.

"We don't just certify text; we anchor the human legacy in a digital world that is becoming increasingly automated."

The Human-Integrity Directive

How HumanProof Works

Our infrastructure is built on three pillars of digital security: Privacy, Immutability, and Verifiability.

01. Cryptographic Privacy

Through local-side SHA-256 hashing, we ensure your intellectual property never leaves your browser. We certify the 'fingerprint', not the file.

02. Atomic Persistence

Every certification is timestamped and recorded in our enterprise cloud layer, creating a permanent, unchangeable record of existence.

03. Global Verification

Professional seals and public IDs allow authors to prove their work's human origin to publishers, legal entities, and academic boards.
