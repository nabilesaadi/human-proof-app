Our Mission

Defending human creativity in the age of generative AI.

HumanProof was founded in 2026 to solve a growing problem: The erosion of trust in digital content. As AI becomes indistinguishable from human writing, authors need a way to prove their work existed before the AI boom.

Our technology uses cryptographic security to create an immutable link between a creator, their content, and a specific point in time.
