Core Infrastructure

Enterprise-Grade Cryptography

We use the same technology that secures global banking and defense systems.

SHA-256 Hashing

We generate a unique 64-character fingerprint of your text. This mathematical function is one-way, meaning your text cannot be recovered from the hash, ensuring 100% privacy.

Atomic Timestamping

Every certification is timestamped against a synchronized global clock. Once the entry is made into our secure database, it is immutable and cannot be altered.

Supabase Cloud

Your records are hosted on high-availability, encrypted enterprise servers, ensuring that your proof is accessible 24/7/365 across the globe.
